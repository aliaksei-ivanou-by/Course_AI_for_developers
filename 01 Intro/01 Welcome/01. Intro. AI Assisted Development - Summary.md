# Module Summary: Introduction to AI-Assisted Development

This module introduces the course's practical approach to AI-assisted software development. It explains who the course is for, why AI skills matter in 2026, and how AI can accelerate coding, learning, prototyping, DevOps, cloud migration, and full-stack delivery. It also presents the course's three core practices—meta prompting, context engineering, and spec-driven development—and previews advanced topics such as multi-agent orchestration, open-source models, and custom harnesses. The central idea is that AI does not replace developers: it helps skilled professionals work and learn more effectively while they remain responsible for engineering decisions and results.
